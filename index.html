Frequently Asked Questions

📦 What Comes with My Order?
Each order includes the WarmRally reclining stadium seat and an instruction card. The seat is powered by any standard USB power bank (not included) so you can bring it anywhere.
📏 How Wide Is the Seat?
The seat is roughly 18" wide and comfortably fits most adults. The backrest reclines into six positions and folds flat to carry like a backpack.
❄️ Can I Use It in Cold Weather?
Yes — that’s exactly what it’s built for. The built-in heating elements warm the seat and back so you stay comfortable at chilly games and outdoor events.
🔥 Does It Heat Both the Seat and Back?
It does. Separate heating zones warm both the seat and the backrest, with multiple heat levels you control from the side panel.
💧 Is WarmRally Water-Resistant?
The outer fabric is water-resistant to handle damp bleachers, light snow and spills. Wipe it clean and let it air dry.
